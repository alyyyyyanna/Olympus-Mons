About

A field guide to Cebu summits, for people who would rather be on the trail.

Motive

Olympus Mons started as a way to keep Cebu’s peaks in one place: a name, a photo, a map pin, and a page you can open before you go. The aim is simple. Spend less time hunting for trail notes, and more time outside.
